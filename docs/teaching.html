Teaching Aptitude

Teaching Aptitude

What is Teaching?

Teaching means helping students learn knowledge, skills and values effectively.

Objectives of Teaching

  • Increase knowledge
  • Develop thinking ability
  • Improve problem solving
  • Build values and behaviour
  • Prepare students for life

Levels of Teaching

1. Memory Level

Students memorize facts and definitions.

Example

Learning formulas and dates.

2. Understanding Level

Students understand concepts and meanings.

Example

Understanding why seasons change.

3. Reflective Level

Students think deeply and solve problems independently.

Example

Finding solutions to pollution problems.

Characteristics of Good Teaching

  • Clear explanation
  • Interactive learning
  • Student-friendly approach
  • Motivation
  • Proper communication

Basic Requirements of Teaching

  1. Teacher
  2. Learner
  3. Subject matter
  4. Communication
  5. Good environment

Learner Characteristics

Adolescent Learners

  • Emotional changes
  • Curious thinking
  • Social interaction

Adult Learners

  • Practical learning
  • Goal-oriented
  • Self-learning

Factors Affecting Teaching

Teacher Related Factors

  • Subject knowledge
  • Communication skill
  • Teaching method

Learner Related Factors

  • Interest
  • Attention
  • Learning ability

Institutional Factors

  • Classroom
  • Library
  • Technology
  • Learning environment

Teaching Methods

Teacher-Centred Method

Teacher explains, students listen.

Learner-Centred Method

Students actively participate.

Online Learning Platforms

  • SWAYAM
  • SWAYAM Prabha
  • MOOCs

Teaching Support System

Traditional

  • Blackboard
  • Chalk
  • Books

Modern

  • Smart boards
  • Projectors

ICT Based

  • Internet
  • Online classes
  • Educational apps

Evaluation System

Evaluation means checking student performance.

Types of Evaluation

Formative Evaluation

Done during learning.

Summative Evaluation

Done at the end of learning.

CBCS

Choice Based Credit System allows students to choose subjects and earn credits flexibly.

Computer Based Testing

Examinations conducted using computers.

Example: UGC NET Examination.

Important Points

  • Reflective level is the highest level of teaching.
  • Learner-centred teaching is more effective.
  • ICT improves modern education.
